The moment

The model worked. Then nothing happened.

At Christian Medical College I rebuilt an MRI pipeline that predicts Alzheimer's years before the first symptom. It worked.

Then it sat there. The research lived in one building and the clinic in another. Nobody who could act on it would ever see it.

That gap is the thing I've been working on since. Not better models. Getting the model into the hands of the person who has to decide.

How I deploy.

Same three moves every time
01

Find the real problem.

Sit with the people doing the work. Pull the data they already have. Write the question down before anything gets built.

02

Ship the first draft.

A working thing in days. Dashboard, agent, model. Good enough to argue with.

03

Measure. Stay until it works.

Before and after, with the number the team actually cares about. Train the people. Fix what they hate.
