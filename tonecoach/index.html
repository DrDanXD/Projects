The five tones and six rules
  1. Third tone is just low. It only dips and rises at the end of a phrase.
  2. 3 + 3 is said as 2 + 3. 你好 is ní hǎo.
  3. Swing wider than feels natural. Natives use 1.5x the range of English.
  4. Light tone is half the length of the syllable before it.
  5. Record yourself. Your ear lies about your own voice.
  6. Ask her to say it back, not to explain it.
